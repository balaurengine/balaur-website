# Privacy

This site does not track you. The engine does not phone home.

## This site

- No analytics, tracking pixels, ads, third-party embeds or cookies.
- No account, newsletter or form.
- `localStorage` only: your light/dark choice and sidebar state. Never sent
  anywhere.
- Search is an index built into the site, queried in your browser.
- The in-browser [editor](/editor) and [benchmarks](/benchmark) run entirely on
  your machine. Nothing you open is uploaded.

## Who else sees a request

- **GitHub Pages** hosts the site and logs requests (IP address, user agent)
  under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Google Fonts** serves the typefaces; your browser fetches them from
  `fonts.googleapis.com` and `fonts.gstatic.com`.

## The engine

- No telemetry, no crash reporting, nothing to opt out of.
- A network request happens only when your game asks for one:
  [`http`](/docs/reference/modules/http),
  [`websocket`](/docs/reference/modules/websocket),
  [Gamend](/docs/manual/gamend).
- Games you ship are yours. So is their privacy policy.
