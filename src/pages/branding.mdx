# Branding

Balaur takes its name from the Romanian dragon and from *Balaur bondoc*, the
stocky dromaeosaur found in Transylvania that carried two sickle claws on each
foot instead of one. The mark is those two claws, drawn in ink with the tips
dipped in one blue wash.

Two versions exist and nothing else: ink claws with blue tips for paper, paper
claws with blue tips for ink. The tile puts the paper version on a rounded
square for favicons and app icons.

- Keep the gap between the claws; it is what separates them at small sizes.
- Use the mark as supplied. No recoloring, no outlines, no rotation, no
  shadows.
- Leave clear space around it of at least a quarter of its height.
- Below 16 pixels use the tile, whose ground keeps the shape readable.

little under the cap height of the type. In running text the name is written
"Balaur", never in capitals.

## Color

One accent, the blue of the wash, on tinted paper or blue-black ink. Nothing
else is saturated.

The same tokens drive the website and the editor's light and dark chrome.

## Type

- **Alegreya**, 600 and 700, for the wordmark, headings and display text.
- **Source Sans 3**, 400 and 600, for running text and interface labels.
- The system monospace for code.

Both faces are free, on [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/share?selection.family=Alegreya:wght@600;700|Source+Sans+3:wght@400;600),
and under the SIL Open Font License.
