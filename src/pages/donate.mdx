# Support Balaur

Balaur is free and MIT. No fees, no revenue share, and nothing held back for a
paid tier. Sponsoring pays for what the engine needs.

## Tiers

Monthly, on [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/balaurengine). One-time
amounts are welcome too, and carry the same role.

| | a month | what it adds |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Supporter | $5 | A sponsor role on [Discord](https://discord.gg/v649emcpAu), and your name on this page |
| Backer | $25 | The `#sponsors` channel: what is being built, and questions answered there |
| Studio | $100 | Your logo and a link on this page |

Each tier carries the ones above it. Being named is a choice, not a default:
say so in the Discord and it happens.

## What it pays for

- Test hardware: Windows, macOS, Linux, a spread of GPUs, real phones.
  Determinism is checked across machines.
- Developer accounts: Apple Developer Program and Google Play, for the
  mobile exports and the [stores module](/docs/manual/stores).
- Code signing: a signed Windows build and a notarised macOS build on
  [Download](/download).
- CI: three operating systems, checksums compared on every push.
- The domain, and time on the [roadmap](/docs/roadmap).

## What it does not buy

- Priority support. Bugs are triaged by how bad they are.
- A say in the roadmap. Ideas go in a
  [Discussion](https://github.com/balaurengine/balaur/discussions), from anyone.
- A licence. MIT already gives you everything; games you make are yours.

For an invoice or a purchase order, ask in the
[Discord](https://discord.gg/v649emcpAu).

## Help that costs nothing

- Run the [examples](/examples) on your hardware. Report what your OS, GPU or
  gamepad did differently.
- Report bugs with the scene or script that reproduces them.
- Fix the docs: every page has an *Edit this page* link.
- Star the [repo](https://github.com/balaurengine/balaur). Tell one person.
