Bones in 2D and 3D, skinned on the GPU, with IK, jiggle, retargeting and ragdolls.

{/* truncate */}

![A skinned leg bending under a clip](/img/editor/persona_animate.webp)

- A bone is a node, and a skin is a polygon with bone weights, painted in the Weights dock.
- `balaur import model.glb` brings in a 3D rig. Both kinds skin on the GPU from a joint palette.

![The column of examples/rig3d bent by its sway clip, its three bones deforming the imported mesh](/img/manual/animation_gpu_skinning.webp)

- `modifier2d` and `modifier3d` run `look_at`, `two_bone_ik`, `fabrik`, `ccdik` and `jiggle` after the clip.

![A modifier's chain, the dashed line to its target, and the handle that drags it](/img/manual/rigging_modifiers.webp)

- A `bone_map` plays a clip made for one skeleton on another, and `physics2d.ragdoll(node)` turns a rig into bodies.

![The Bone map dock: canonical names on the left, the rig's own bones on the right](/img/manual/rigging_bonemap.webp)
