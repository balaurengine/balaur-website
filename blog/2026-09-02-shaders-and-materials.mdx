## What landed

- Shaders. WESL (WGSL plus imports, `@if` variants and dead-code
  elimination), linked to WGSL at run time. A 2D shader imports
  `package::sprite`. A 3D one imports `package::mesh` and calls `shade(in)`
  for the scene's lights.
- Materials. A `material` asset names a shader and its `[params]`, read off
  the linked shader. `sprite`, `shape2d`, `shape3d` and `mesh` take one. A
  node without one draws with the built-in material.
- Relink on save. Saving a shader relinks every material naming it and
  rebuilds every node drawing with it. A shader that will not link is
  reported with its source line; the node keeps the built-in material.
- Channels. `render::set_channel("uv")` draws the scene's UVs; `normals`,
  `depth` and `albedo` are the others.

```toml
# materials/glow.toml
type = "material"
shader = "shaders/glow.wesl"

[params]
speed = 2.0
rings = 5.0
tint = "#ffb347"
```

A shader is outside the digest. `time()` is render time, not the tick. A
function marked `@const` runs on the CPU in headless tests.

`examples/shaders` is a glow material on a sprite beside a plain one. It
[runs in the browser](/examples/?play=shaders).
