FABRIK, CCDIK and jiggle in 2D and 3D, deform tracks that key vertices,
retargeting through a bone map, and ragdolls built from a rig. The editor
gets a Weights dock, mirroring and trace to go with them.

{/* truncate */}

- `modifier2d` gained `fabrik`, `ccdik` and `jiggle`. Jiggle has no target;
  it springs each bone toward the pose the clip left. `modifier3d` has the
  same five kinds.

  ![A modifier's chain, the dashed line to its target, and the handle that drags it](/img/manual/rigging_modifiers.webp)

- `polygon/deform` keys an offset per vertex, added before skinning.

- A `bone_map` pairs canonical bone names with the paths they take on one
  rig. A rotation key is read as a turn away from the profile's rest, so a
  clip built for another skeleton plays on this one.

  ![The Bone map dock: canonical names on the left, the rig's own bones on the right](/img/manual/rigging_bonemap.webp)

- `physics2d.ragdoll(node)` builds a body and a capsule per bone from a rig,
  each hinged to its parent's. A `blend` mixes the clip's pose with the
  simulated one.

- The Weights dock has Bind, Unbind, Normalise, Auto and Smooth. Both tools
  mirror, Trace walks a texture's alpha, and bone names show in the
  viewport.

The [Animation manual](/docs/manual/animation) has the tables and the TOML.
