Completion, hover, go to definition, rename and formatting, in the editor and over `balaur lsp`.

{/* truncate */}

![The completion popup under the caret, on a module path](/img/manual/script_completion.webp)

- After `physics2d::` completion lists that module, and after `node.body2d.` what acts on that component.
- `⌘B` goes to a definition, `⌘⇧R` renames across files, and `⌥⇧F` formats.
- The Problems dock checks on save for a missing script, prefab or asset, and for a shader that will not link.
- A VS Code extension spawns `balaur lsp`, which runs the same checks.
