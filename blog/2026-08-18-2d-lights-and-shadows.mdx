`light2d` and `occluder2d` build a light map every sprite, polygon and tile is
multiplied by. Shadows come from the shapes a node has.

{/* truncate */}

- `light2d` is point or directional, with `color`, `radius`, `intensity` and
  `shadows`. A point light fades quadratically to zero at `radius`, and
  unlit parts fall to the camera's `ambient`.
- An `occluder2d` left empty follows the node's `collider2d`, or its circle,
  capsule, rect or sprite. A directional light casts parallel shadows.

  ![Three occluders under one directional light, each casting a parallel strip](/img/manual/rendering_directional.webp)

- `post = ["bloom"]` on the camera picks up an `intensity` past 1. `ssao`,
  `ssr` and `dof` are the 3D passes.
- In the editor, a Lights chip draws every light's reach and every
  occluder's resolved outline, and the Polygon tool traces an occluder in
  Points mode.

  ![The editor with an occluder selected: the Points chip, an Occluder section, light gizmos over the lit scene](/img/manual/rendering_occluder_tool.webp)

```toml
[nodes.light2d]
kind = "point"
color = "#ffd28a"
radius = 8.0
intensity = 1.6
shadows = true
```

Lights are outside the simulation. A scene with no `light2d` adds no pass. A
frame draws at most 64 lights.
[Rendering](/docs/manual/rendering) has the rest.
