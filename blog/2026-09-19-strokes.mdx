A `shape2d` polyline is one outline, with the corners and ends you pick.

{/* truncate */}

![Seven polylines: round, miter and bevel joins, butt, square and round caps, a tapered line with a gradient, and two closed squares](/img/blog/strokes.webp)

## Joins and caps

- `join` is `round`, `miter` or `bevel`, and `cap` is `round`, `butt` or `square`.
- `miter_limit` is how far a miter may reach, in half-widths, before the corner is cut to a bevel. The default is 4, as in SVG.
- `taper = [1.0, 0.05]` narrows a line from its full `width` at the start to 5% of it at the end.

## One outline

- `i_overlay` strokes the whole chain in integer math, and the triangulator fills it in fixed point.
- No two triangles of a stroke overlap, so a translucent line blends once, joints included.
- A `gradient` steps through 32 bands along the line's length.

```toml
[nodes.shape2d]
kind = "polyline"
mesh = "#zig"
width = 0.6
taper = [1.0, 0.05]
color = [1.0, 0.5, 0.2, 1.0]
gradient = [1.0, 1.0, 0.3, 1.0]
```
