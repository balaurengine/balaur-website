finger.

![The same editor on a desktop, a tablet and a phone](/img/blog/one_editor_three_screens.webp)

## Docks

- On a tablet both side docks start folded, and an open one takes half the
  screen.
- On a phone all three start folded, and one opens at a time.
- A folded dock leaves a handle at its edge, and an open one folds back the
  same way.

![A phone with nothing open, then the outliner, the inspector and the output](/img/blog/phone_one_panel.webp)

## Touch

- Under a finger the glyph, the plate and the bar grow together.
- Every control is at least 44 points, and the editor's selftest fails one
  under that.
