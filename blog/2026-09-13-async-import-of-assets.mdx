Importing an asset runs on its own thread, so the editor keeps drawing.

{/* truncate */}

![The editor importing two files at once, one finished and one part way through](/img/manual/editor_import.webp)

- A saved texture, model, font or sound reloads, and every node built from it is rebuilt.
- An `id://` reference survives a move, and a rename in the Assets dock rewrites every reference to the file.
- `art/hero.png.toml` holds one image's import settings, and `[import.texture]` in `project.toml` is the default.
- `filter = "nearest"` draws pixel art crisp.

![The same 32-pixel sprite at ten times its size, filtered linearly and with nearest](/img/manual/import_settings.webp)
