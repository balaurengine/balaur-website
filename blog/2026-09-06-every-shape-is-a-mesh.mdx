Ten 3D primitives and six 2D ones. Bezier paths extruded, revolved and swept.
Booleans in both dimensions. A cloner. Text as geometry.

{/* truncate */}

## What landed

- Primitives. Torus, pyramid, prism, tube, rounded cuboid; ellipse, star
  and ngon in 2D. `segments`, `rings`, `sides`, `corner_radius` on all of them.

  ![The primitives row](/img/manual/objects_primitives.webp)

- Paths. `path2d` and `path3d` assets. Extrude with a bevel, lathe, sweep
  along a rail, stroke.

  ![An arch extruded, a goblet revolved, a rail swept](/img/manual/objects_paths.webp)

- Booleans. `boolean3d` and `boolean2d` over a node's children: union,
  difference, intersection. Operands stay editable; moving one recomputes.
  Booleans nest.

  ![Union, difference and intersection of a cube and a ball](/img/manual/objects_booleans.webp)

- In 2D. `shape2d` gained `ellipse`, `star` and `ngon`. `boolean2d` joins,
  cuts and intersects filled outlines. The 2D booleans run on `i_overlay`,
  in fixed-point integers: the same vertices on every platform.

  ![Ellipse, star, ngon and capsule; a rect and a circle joined, cut and intersected](/img/manual/objects_flat.webp)

- Cloner. Linear, radial, grid. `seed` and `random` scatter the copies. A
  thousand copies are one draw call, custom materials included.

  ![A row, a ring and a scattered grid](/img/manual/objects_cloner.webp)

- Text. `kind = "text"` on a `mesh` asset: a word as geometry, counters
  kept as holes, sized in world units, sitting on its baseline.

- Colours and morph targets. Both survive a glTF import. A morph weight is
  a property; a clip drives it as `mesh/morph.<name>`. Vertex colours reach
  a material with `features = { vertex_color = true }`.

  ![A word as a mesh, and a triangle coloured per vertex](/img/manual/objects_text.webp)

## One mesher

- A collider fitted to a torus is a torus.
- A ray picks the shape that is drawn.
- Headless tests check every mesh is closed and outward-facing, with the
  exact volume a boolean should have.
- Deterministic: `libm` for the curves, and a BSP of dot products and lerps.
- The meshers are the engine's own: core builds the triangles, physics fits
  its colliders to them.

```toml
[[assets]]
id = "ring"
type = "mesh"
kind = "torus"
radius = 1.1
tube_radius = 0.28

[nodes.mesh]
source = "#ring"

[nodes.collider3d]
kind = "trimesh"
mesh = "#ring"
```

`examples/objects` has five rows, one per category, then the 2D layer. It
runs [in the browser](/examples/?play=objects). The
[manual](/docs/manual/scenes#objects) and the generated
[asset](/docs/reference/assets) and [component](/docs/reference/components)
references have the keys.
