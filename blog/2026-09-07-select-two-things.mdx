The editor's selection is a set, with box select, group, align and
distribute. A scene owns its `light3d` nodes and an `environment`, there is
a `pbr` material, and event bindings live in the scene file.

![The library dock, with a torus selected in the objects example](/img/manual/editor_selection.webp)

## Selection sets

- Shift and ⌘ click add to the set, and a marquee on empty viewport
  box-selects in both 2D and 3D. A gizmo drag moves, turns and scales the
  whole set about its own centre.
- Group makes a parent at the centroid and keeps every world pose. There
  are six aligns and three distributes.
- `H` hides, `⌘L` locks, `/` isolates. Hidden writes `visible`; locked is
  editor state the gizmo and box select skip.
- The inspector edits the set. A property write reaches every selected node
  carrying that component.

## Lights and environment

- `light3d` is point, directional or spot, with shadows and light layers. A
  scene with none keeps the engine's key light, and the first `light3d`
  retires it.
- `environment` holds sky, ambient, fog, exposure, tonemap, grading and the
  shadow budget on one component. The last `current` one wins.
- `package::pbr` is GGX, Smith and Schlick over the frame's lights, with six
  texture slots and a one-pixel neutral for every slot a material omits.

## Event bindings

- `[variables]` holds a value, `states` names a node's looks, and
  `[[nodes.bindings.rows]]` join them.
- `examples/hello` opens a door on the third click this way, with no script.
- The Events view writes the rows. "Convert to script" writes the Rune they
  mean.

![The Events view: two rows on the ball, one counting the click and one opening the door](/img/manual/editor_events.webp)

Also in this one: drag a file onto the window and it becomes a node, and a
Pen tool edits a `path2d`. A Cost dock shows draws, triangles and texture
bytes per node. There is a Library of materials, lighting setups and
templates, and `balaur new --template`.
