A selection set with box select, group, align and distribute. `light3d` and an
`environment` a scene owns. A `pbr` material. Event bindings in the scene
file.

{/* truncate */}

![The library dock, with a torus selected in the objects example](/img/manual/editor_selection.webp)

## Selection sets

- **Box select and modifiers.** Shift and ⌘ click, a marquee on empty
  viewport in both 2D and 3D. A gizmo drag moves, turns and scales the whole
  set about its own centre.
- **Group, align, distribute.** Group makes a parent at the centroid and
  keeps every world pose. Six aligns, three distributes.
- **Hide, lock, isolate.** `H`, `⌘L`, `/`. Hidden writes `visible`; locked is
  editor state the gizmo and box select skip.
- **The inspector edits the set.** A property write reaches every selected
  node carrying that component.

## Lights and environment

- **`light3d`.** Point, directional and spot, with shadows and light layers.
  A scene with none keeps the engine's key light; the first `light3d`
  retires it.
- **`environment`.** Sky, ambient, fog, exposure, tonemap, grading and the
  shadow budget, on one component. The last `current` one wins.
- **`package::pbr`.** GGX, Smith and Schlick over the frame's lights. Six
  texture slots, and a one-pixel neutral for every slot a material omits.

## Event bindings

- `[variables]` holds a value, `states` names a node's looks, and
  `[[nodes.bindings]]` rows join them.
- `examples/hello` opens a door on the third click this way, with no script.
- The Events view writes the rows. "Convert to script" writes the Rune they
  mean.

![The Events view: two rows on the ball, one counting the click and one opening the door](/img/manual/editor_events.webp)
