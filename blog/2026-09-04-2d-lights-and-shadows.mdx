`light2d` and `occluder2d` build a light map every sprite, polygon and tile is
multiplied by. Shadows come from the shapes a node has.

{/* truncate */}


## What landed

- `light2d`. Point or directional, with `color`, `radius`, `intensity` and
  `shadows`. A point light fades quadratically to zero at `radius`. Unlit
  parts fall to the camera's `ambient`.
- `occluder2d`. Left empty, the outline follows the node's `collider2d`,
  or its circle, capsule, rect or sprite. A directional light casts
  parallel shadows.

  ![Three occluders under one directional light, each casting a parallel strip](/img/manual/rendering_directional.webp)

- Bloom. `post = ["bloom"]` on the camera; it picks up an `intensity` past
  1. `ssao`, `ssr` and `dof` are the 3D passes.
- Editor. A Lights chip draws every light's reach and every occluder's
  resolved outline. The Polygon tool traces an occluder in Points mode.

  ![The editor with an occluder selected: the Points chip, an Occluder section, light gizmos over the lit scene](/img/manual/rendering_occluder_tool.webp)

```toml
[nodes.light2d]
kind = "point"
color = "#ffd28a"
radius = 8.0
intensity = 1.6
shadows = true
```

Lights are outside the simulation. A scene with no `light2d` adds no pass. A
frame draws at most 64 lights.
[Rendering](/docs/manual/rendering) has the rest.
